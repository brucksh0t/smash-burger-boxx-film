Next door to the bagels

It started as a lunch special.

The smash burger began as a Wednesday-to-Friday lunch special at Hudson Bagels. It got its own red-and-white box at 93 Ten Broeck Ave, run by the same family: a seasonal stand for smash burgers, fries and milkshakes.
